You Have Been Phished!

Don't panic. This was a classroom simulation.

🎯 What just happened?

You clicked a link inside a simulated phishing email. Nothing was downloaded, no password was collected, and no personal information was recorded.

🔎 What clues could you have spotted?

🚨 Urgency The message tried to make you act quickly without giving you time to think.
🔗 Suspicious Link Always check where a link actually goes before clicking it.
📧 Sender Check the sender's email address carefully. Small differences can be a warning sign.
🔐 Account Request Be cautious when an unexpected message asks you to verify your account or password.

💡 The Golden Rule

STOP → THINK → CHECK

Before clicking a suspicious link, stop and inspect the message, sender, and destination.
