?? You Have Been Phished!

Don't worry — this was a classroom simulation.

This was a cybersecurity awareness exercise designed to demonstrate how easy it can be to fall for a phishing email.

What should you have noticed?

• The message created a sense of urgency.
• It asked you to click a link.
• The sender or link may not have been trustworthy.
• The message asked you to verify your account.

Think before you click!
Check the sender, inspect links, and never enter your password after following an unexpected email link.
